Islamicate Caucasus & Central Asia -- Candidate City List
==========================================================

Potential additions to the Islamic CarmenWorld seed data for
cities in the Caucasus and Central Asia with significant
Islamicate cultural heritage: mosques, madrasas, Silk Road
caravanserais, Timurid architecture, Sufi shrines, and
living Muslim tradition shaping civic identity.

Current coverage: Tashkent is the only Central Asian city
(connected to Isfahan and Lahore). No Caucasus cities exist.


Caucasus
--------

- Baku (Azerbaijan)      -- TOP PICK
  Icherisheher (Old City, UNESCO), Palace of the Shirvanshahs,
  Bibi-Heybat Mosque, Heydar Aliyev Center, Flame Towers.
  Majority Shia Muslim country; dramatic medieval/ultra-modern
  contrast; Caspian Sea port; "Land of Fire" Zoroastrian legacy;
  carpet-weaving; mugham music.
  Coords: 40.41, 49.87
  Graph connections: Istanbul (flight, Turkic corridor),
    Isfahan (Caspian/Persian link), Tashkent (Central Asian corridor)
  Role: Hub/connector filling void between Turkey and Central Asia

- Derbent (Dagestan, Russia) -- niche/adventurous pick
  Naryn-Kala Citadel (UNESCO), Juma Mosque (oldest in Russia),
  Old Town, Derbent Wall.
  "Caspian Gates" (Alexander the Great legend); Sassanid-era
  fortifications; multicultural Dagestan (40+ ethnic groups);
  ancient trade-route chokepoint between steppe and Persia.
  Graph connections: Baku (natural pair), Istanbul (flight)
  Downside: Obscure; limited to 2-3 connections; better as a
  spoke/dead-end off Baku.

- Sheki (Azerbaijan)     -- secondary pick
  Palace of Sheki Khans (UNESCO), shebeke stained-glass craft,
  18th-century caravanserai.
  Downside: Small town, fewer site options, better as a spoke
  off Baku than a standalone city.


Central Asia
------------

- Samarkand (Uzbekistan) -- TOP PICK
  Registan Square, Shah-i-Zinda necropolis, Bibi-Khanym Mosque,
  Gur-e-Amir (Timur's tomb), Siab Bazaar.
  Timur/Tamerlane's capital; Silk Road crossroads; Ulugh Beg's
  observatory (medieval astronomy); blue tile mosaics;
  paper-making tradition (oldest in Islamic world).
  Coords: 39.65, 66.96
  Graph connections: Tashkent (train/high-speed rail, ~300 km),
    Isfahan (Timurid-Safavid cultural link),
    Lahore (Mughal connection -- Babur came through),
    possibly Baku or Bukhara
  Role: Connector; bridges Tashkent into richer sub-graph

- Bukhara (Uzbekistan)   -- strong second
  Po-i-Kalyan complex (Kalyan Minaret + mosque), Ark Citadel,
  Lyab-i-Hauz ensemble, Samanid Mausoleum (~10th c., oldest
  Islamic monument in the region), covered bazaars (Taki-Zargaron).
  "Bukhara the Noble"; Imam al-Bukhari's birthplace (compiler of
  most authoritative hadith collection); Avicenna's homeland;
  Naqshbandi Sufi order; "Genghis Khan burned it, Timur rebuilt it."
  Coords: 39.77, 64.42
  Graph connections: Tashkent (train), Samarkand (train, same
    Silk Road artery); spoke off Tashkent or Samarkand
  Role: Deepens Central Asian cluster (Tashkent-Samarkand-Bukhara
    triangle) for multi-hop chases

- Khiva (Uzbekistan)     -- atmospheric but peripheral
  Ichan Kala walled city (entire inner city is UNESCO), Kalta Minor
  (unfinished turquoise minaret), Juma Mosque (218 carved wooden
  columns), Tash-Hauli Palace.
  Slave trade history; Khorezm empire; desert oasis;
  Al-Khwarizmi's birthplace (father of algebra -- the word
  "algorithm" derives from his name).
  Coords: 41.38, 60.36
  Graph connections: Spoke off Bukhara or Tashkent; dead-end risk
    unless Turkmenistan cities are added
  Role: Spoke/dead-end


Recommendations for the Graph
------------------------------

Highest-impact additions:

  City        | Region       | Role           | Natural connections
  ------------+--------------+----------------+-------------------------------
  Baku        | Caucasus     | Hub/connector  | Istanbul, Isfahan, Tashkent
  Samarkand   | Central Asia | Connector      | Tashkent, Isfahan, Lahore/Baku

This turns the Isfahan--Tashkent corridor into:
  - Isfahan -- Baku -- Istanbul  (Caucasus arc)
  - Isfahan -- Tashkent -- Samarkand  (Central Asian arc)

Bukhara is a strong third for a deeper Central Asian cluster
(Tashkent--Samarkand--Bukhara triangle).

If only one city: Samarkand gives the most bang -- iconic enough
to anchor clues, and it bridges Tashkent (currently a leaf node
with only 2 connections) into a more interesting sub-graph.
