# Characters: Carmen Sandiego — Islamicate Edition

## The Villain Organization: F.I.T.N.A.

*Federation of International Thieves and Nefarious Agents*

("Fitna" means trial, temptation, or intrigue in Arabic — perfect for a criminal syndicate.)

---

## The Mastermind

**Qamar Samarkandi** — The Carmen figure. "Qamar" means moon; Samarkand is one of the great Silk Road cities. She's elegant, erudite, always quoting poetry before vanishing. Trained as a conservator at the Islamic Arts Museum in Doha before turning to a life of theft. Her calling card: a crescent moon sticker left at every crime scene.

## The Good Guys

**Agent Amira Battuta** — A young investigator named in spirit after Ibn Battuta, the greatest traveler in pre-modern history. Smart, resourceful, multilingual. Specializes in cultural heritage cases across South and Southeast Asia.

**Agent Khalid Battuta** — Amira's counterpart and fellow field agent. Calm under pressure, with a photographic memory for architectural details and trade routes. Covers the Middle East, North Africa, and Central Asian beat.

**The Qadi** — The Chief equivalent. A retired Interpol judge ("qadi" = Islamic judge) who runs the task force from a secure office. Stern but fair, delivers briefings with dry wit.

**Shahrazad** — The informant network. Named after the narrator of the 1001 Nights, she's actually a distributed network of local contacts — shopkeepers, cab drivers, tea house regulars — who piece together stories from fragments. Each city has its own Shahrazad contact.

**Professor Biruni** — The tech and research expert. Named after al-Biruni, the medieval polymath. Provides historical and geographic context for each case. The one who explains what was stolen and why it matters.
